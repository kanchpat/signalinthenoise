Signal 1: The Dawn of the Agentic Economy (Machine-to-Machine Commerce)

We’ve spent the last three years talking about how AI will help humans do their jobs better. We missed the actual plot. 

The media focuses on agents acting as personal assistants. The actual signal is that agents are beginning to transact autonomously. Financial institutions like Santander are deploying infrastructure for agent-initiated payments, and China is working on AI token futures. We are transitioning into a machine-to-machine economy where AI agents independently negotiate, purchase, and execute services.

In this episode, we break down why the human is no longer the primary consumer, and why you must optimize your business for agentic transactions.
