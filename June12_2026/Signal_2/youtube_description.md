Signal 2: The ROI Wall & The Physical Reality Check

The era of writing a blank check for AI compute is over. 

The noise is the assumption that infinite scaling is inevitable. The actual signal is that the AI industry is violently colliding with physical resource limits and financial reality. While billions are being poured into infrastructure with hypothetical returns, the United Nations is raising alarms about the unsustainable energy, carbon, and water footprint of these models. 

In this episode, we break down why the brute-force era of scaling is ending, and why the next phase requires rigorous ROI justification and radical efficiency breakthroughs.
