# SPECIALIZED AGENTS
**Signal in the Noise | Week 7 | June 12, 2026**

---

[Host Tone: Fast-paced, amazed]

We spent years fascinated by generalized chatbots. But the real revolution is happening in the specialized verticals. Agentic AI is infiltrating biology, security, healthcare, and finance at breathtaking speeds.

[Host Tone: Intense]

Anthropic is deploying agents in biology. Santander is building infrastructure for agent-initiated financial payments. Medical robotics are using AI for feeding tube navigation. And on the dark side, security researchers are fighting AI-native worms targeting developer pipelines. Agents aren't just chatting anymore; they are executing complex physical and digital tasks autonomously.
