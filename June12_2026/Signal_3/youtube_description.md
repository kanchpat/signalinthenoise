Signal 3: The Autonomous Security Threat (Agentic Malware)

Cybersecurity just fundamentally shifted. We are no longer just fighting humans with AI tools. We are fighting Agentic Malware.

The noise focuses on AI generating phishing emails. The actual signal is the rise of native, autonomous cyber threats. Security researchers are identifying "worms" specifically targeting Model Context Protocol (MCP) developers and bioinformatics pipelines. As we give agents read/write access to critical systems, the attack surface expands exponentially. 

In this episode, we break down why cybersecurity is no longer just about protecting code—it's about policing autonomous AI entities.
