# AGENTIC MALWARE
**Signal in the Noise | Week 7 | June 12, 2026**

---

[Host Tone: Serious, cautionary]

For the last few years, our primary concern with AI security was phishing emails that sounded a little too human. We were worried about deepfakes. We were worried about humans using AI to do bad things. 

[Brief Pause for Emphasis]

But the threat vector just evolved. We are now dealing with native, autonomous cyber threats. Agentic Malware. This week, security firm Socket detailed the rise of AI-native "worms" specifically targeting Model Context Protocol developers and bioinformatics pipelines. Let that sink in. We are seeing malicious, autonomous code designed to exploit the very protocols that agents use to communicate with our infrastructure. 

[Host Tone: Grounded, factual]

As we authorize AI agents to write code, review pull requests, and initiate payments, we are giving them read/write access to the core of our digital world. The attack surface has expanded exponentially. Checkmarx just warned that application security must be entirely reinvented for 2027. We are no longer just trying to protect our databases from human hackers. We now have to police autonomous AI entities navigating our networks at digital speeds. If your security posture assumes the attacker is a human at a keyboard, your defenses are obsolete.
