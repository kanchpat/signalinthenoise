# THE PHYSICAL TOLL
**Signal in the Noise | Week 7 | June 12, 2026**

---

[Host Tone: Grave, urgent]

We talk about AI as if it exists in the cloud. But the cloud is just someone else's physical computer. And right now, the infrastructure build-out is hitting the hard limits of our planet. Nvidia and LG are building massive AI factories. 

[Host Tone: Emphatic]

But the United Nations just issued a severe warning regarding the environmental cost of AI. We are talking about staggering, unsustainable energy consumption, carbon emissions, and water footprints. The hardware boom is violently colliding with the physical constraints of the Earth.
