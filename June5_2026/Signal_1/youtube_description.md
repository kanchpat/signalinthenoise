Signal 1: The Transition to Agentic Engineering & Organizational Redesign

Your AI budget is growing. Your returns aren't. Welcome to the ROI Wall.

The media focuses on AI chatbots writing snippets of code. The actual signal is that engineering itself is becoming an autonomous, closed-loop system. Autonomous agents are now driving end-to-end deployments across the entire software lifecycle — and the primary bottleneck to AI ROI is no longer the technology. It's your org chart.

In this episode, we break down how Salesforce, OpenAI, and Bain & Company are all converging on the same conclusion: the companies that win the next 24 months are the ones redesigning their organizations around agentic workflows. The human is no longer the doer. The human is the director.
