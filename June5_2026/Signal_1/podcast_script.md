# THE ROI WALL
**Signal in the Noise | Week 6 | June 5, 2026**

---

[Host Tone: Confident, unhurried]

Are you throwing more money at AI and getting less back? You're not alone. Welcome to the ROI Wall. 

[Brief Pause for Emphasis]

Over the last 18 months, every company on the planet rushed to buy AI subscriptions. They handed out ChatGPT licenses, they integrated basic copilots, and they expected productivity to skyrocket. But right now, boardrooms are having a very uncomfortable conversation. Bain & Company just released a critical report detailing exactly why your AI budget is growing, but your returns aren't. And the uncomfortable truth? It’s not a failure of the technology. It’s a failure of your organizational chart.

We are seeing a massive, fundamental shift away from the "AI as an assistant" paradigm. We are moving into the era of what Salesforce is formally calling "Agentic Engineering." Now, what does that actually mean? It means we are no longer using tools to help a human write a snippet of code. We are authorizing autonomous agents to drive end-to-end deployments across the entire software development lifecycle. These agents are writing the code, reviewing their own pull requests, testing the software, and pushing it to production. They operate in a closed loop. 

[Host Tone: Leaning in, serious]

Here is why this breaks legacy companies: if you take a highly autonomous, high-speed agent and bolt it onto a sluggish, bureaucratic 2015 workflow, you don't get 10x productivity. You get a 10x faster traffic jam. You are paying for premium compute power to sit idle while waiting for human sign-offs at five different middle-management layers. 

The companies that will dominate the next 24 months are not the ones spending the most on compute. They are the ones aggressively tearing down their legacy corporate structures to build native, agent-first workflows. The entire concept of human capital is shifting. The human is no longer the doer—the executioner of tasks. The human is now the director, the orchestrator defining the boundaries and ethics of the protocol. 

If your company is still treating AI like a really smart intern, you are bleeding money. You need to stop optimizing your human workers with AI, and start optimizing your AI workflows with human judgment.
