Signal 2: Model Evolution — Spatial Intelligence and On-Device Constraints

The AI race was a brute-force contest of scale. The game just changed.

The noise is obsessed with parameter counts and data center size. The actual signal is a simultaneous push toward two entirely new frontiers: grounding models in the physical world (spatial intelligence) and violently compressing them to run locally on the edge. We are entering the era of Large World Models and hyper-compressed edge intelligence.

In this episode, we break down how World Labs, Google, and Anthropic are converging on a future where AI isn't just a cloud service you rent — it's a locally hosted, spatially aware entity that understands the physical world around you. And it's starting to build itself.
